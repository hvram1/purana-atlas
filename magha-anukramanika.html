माघमासः

The month of Māgha

A month of daily talks, each on one observance of Māgha — a tithi, a festival, the bath at Prayāga — and each expounded from the book that tells it. There is no single text under this series: its 26 days draw on 13 divisions of the purāṇas and the Mahābhārata. So this is not an atlas of one edition but an index to the recordings: every verse, named, placed to the second.

Two Māgha series, two shapes

The Padma Purāṇa has a Māgha-māhātmya of its own — Uttarakhaṇḍa 125–129 — and Musiri Yagnarama Somayaji Dīkṣitar’s Māgha Snānam recites it in order. That series has a verse atlas: माघमाहात्म्यम्. The series on this page does not walk that text; 3 of its rows fall on it. It walks the month instead.
